Gavin "Kerple" O'Hanlon

Gavin KO is a artist and game developer, born and raised on the Jersey shore and now based in Long Island, New York. His artistic journey began when he started building video games as a child, learning how to infuse creative expression into the gameplay and create art and music to bring his visions for the games to life. Finding a love of all things artistic, Gavin began painting as creative outlet free from the corporatism of video game development. As an artist, Gavin has a passion for crafting works that bring his viewers in tune with their messy, chaotic, and beautifully human emotions, and inspire them to think deeply about politics, society, and the worlds they inhabit.
